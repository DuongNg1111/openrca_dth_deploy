The mission

OpenRCA (ICLR'25) asks software systems: given a failure and its telemetry, what is the root cause? It has 335 real failures and 68 GB of metrics, logs and traces.

The opportunity: the best LLM today solves only ~11% of cases. That gap is our project — build a working RCA system, add one novel improvement, run experiments, and write a short research paper.

MissionCapstone → paper

What is Root Cause Analysis (RCA)?

Telemetrymetrics (KPIs) · logs · traces

Questionwhich component failed & why?
→
Answercomponent + reason + time

Example question:

“On 2021-03-25 between 09:00 and 09:30 the delivery-tracking service showed elevated errors. Find the root cause component and reason.”

"root cause component": "order-service",
"root cause reason": "high disk I/O read usage"

Our system: Logistics / Delivery Platform RCA

We frame the OpenRCA Market system as a Logistics / Delivery Platform RCA. Our "services" are:

frontend-gatewayorder-serviceshipment-servicetracking-servicewarehouse-servicepayment-service

When one of these breaks, our system must point to the right one and explain the reason — automatically, from telemetry.

Where the paper (and novelty) comes from

  • Heuristic + LLM hybrid that triages cheaply and only calls the LLM on hard cases (cut cost & latency).
  • Trace-graph-aware retrieval that walks the service dependency graph to localize the failing service.
  • Domain-transfer framing of microservice RCA as a logistics fulfillment pipeline.

We pick one in M5 and prove it beats the baseline in M6.
